DEFINITIONS & LIMITATIONS

Make the numbers explainable.

01 / Consistent boundaries

The period includes its start date and excludes its end date. Hire date is the first active day. Exit date is the first inactive day. Opening and closing headcounts are measured immediately before events on their boundary dates.

02 / A defined denominator

Average headcount equals active employee-days divided by calendar days. Turnover equals exits divided by that average, multiplied by 100. Rates cover the selected period and are not annualised. A zero denominator displays N/A.

03 / Honest scope

Department, location and FTE stay fixed within each employee record. Transfers, rehires, leave, historic FTE changes and reasons for leaving are outside this demonstration. Patterns do not prove causes or predict individual behaviour.
